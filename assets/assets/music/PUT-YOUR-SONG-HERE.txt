Put your song in this folder and name it exactly:

    our-song.mp3

(or use another name and change "musicPath" in src/data/anniversary-data.ts)

Tips:
- Use a song you own or that is royalty-free / properly licensed.
- A calm instrumental (piano or strings) works best, because the apology and
  final pages are read quietly. The player lowers the volume automatically there.
- Keep the file under about 8 MB so it loads quickly on phones.
- If the file is missing, the website still works - the music button just shows
  that no song is available.
